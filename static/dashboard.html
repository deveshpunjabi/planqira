From first idea to event day

Planqira helps you see what your event will cost and what needs to happen next.

  1. Choose an event. Explore a sample from the sidebar, or create your own.
  2. Review the budget. Estimates help you plan; approved quotes record what you've agreed.
  3. Try the quote assistant. Paste a vendor message or load an example. Check every extracted term before approval.
  4. Explore changes. Try a different guest count and preview the cost. Nothing changes until you save.
  5. Prepare for the day. Work through the checklist, follow the schedule, and download your plan.

For a quick presentation: overview → budget → quote example → preview a guest-count change. Quote reading may use a basic parser when AI is unavailable.
